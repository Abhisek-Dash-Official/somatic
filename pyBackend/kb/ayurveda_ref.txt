[Vata Dosha]: Characterized by dry skin, joint pain, anxiety, irregular digestion. Balanced by warm, grounding foods, sesame oil massage, and routine.
[Pitta Dosha]: Characterized by acidity, inflammation, anger, heat intolerance, sharp appetite. Balanced by cooling foods, sweet fruits, avoiding spicy/fried items.
[Kapha Dosha]: Characterized by lethargy, congestion, weight gain, slow digestion, oily skin. Balanced by light, warm, spicy foods, regular vigorous exercise, and waking up early.
