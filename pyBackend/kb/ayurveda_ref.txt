DATA PROVENANCE POLICY
1. Official AYUSH/API material is preferred for identity, drug dossiers, standardization and research.
2. Classical properties are retained as Ayurvedic/traditional knowledge and must not be represented as modern clinical efficacy.
3. Secondary academic sources may be used for gap-filling only when a primary source is not yet retrieved; they are marked as such.
4. Dosage and patient-specific treatment recommendations are excluded from this corpus unless separately verified from an authoritative source.
5. Every production chunk must retain its source, evidence type and review status.

[ROGA-001] Jvara
Ayurvedic name: Jvara
English crosswalk: Fever syndrome / Pyrexia
Nidana (Causes): Mithya Ahara Vihara (improper diet/lifestyle), Ritu Viparyaya (seasonal variations), Abhighata (trauma).
Purvarupa (Prodromal): Shrama (fatigue), Aruchi (anorexia), Gatra Gaurava (heaviness of body), Klama (exhaustion without exertion).
Rupa (Symptoms): Santapa (elevated body temperature), Aruchi, Trishna (excessive thirst), Angamarda (body ache), Svedavarodha (obstruction of sweat).
Samprapti (Pathogenesis): Aggravated Doshas (mainly Pitta) enter the Amashaya, displace the Jatharagni (digestive fire) to the exterior (Shakha), causing Santapa (heat) and obstructing sweat channels.
Dosha: Tridosha (Pitta predominance).
Dushya: Rasa Dhatu.
Srotas: Rasavaha, Swedavaha.

[ROGA-002] Kasa
Ayurvedic name: Kasa
English crosswalk: Cough syndrome
Nidana (Causes): Dhuma (smoke exposure), Raja (dust), Vyayama (excessive exertion), Ruksha anna (dry food consumption), Vegadharana (suppression of natural urges).
Purvarupa (Prodromal): Kantha kandu (itching in the throat), Bhojyana aushadha arodha (difficulty in swallowing food).
Rupa (Symptoms): Shushka or Ardra Kasa (dry or productive cough), Uras shula (chest pain), Swarabheda (hoarseness of voice).
Samprapti (Pathogenesis): Prana and Udana Vayu get obstructed by Kapha, moving in an upward direction, producing a sound like a broken bronze vessel.
Dosha: Vata (Prana, Udana) and Kapha.
Dushya: Rasa, Prana.
Srotas: Pranavaha.

[ROGA-003] Shwasa
Ayurvedic name: Śvāsa
English crosswalk: Dyspnea / Asthma syndrome
Nidana (Causes): Exposure to dust and smoke, cold water intake, Marmaghata (injury to vital organs), Ama accumulation, secondary to Kasa or Jvara.
Purvarupa (Prodromal): Anaha (abdominal distension), Parshva shula (flank pain), Prana vilomata (upward movement of breath).
Rupa (Symptoms): Breathing difficulty, rapid respiration, wheezing, orthopnea, chest tightness.
Samprapti (Pathogenesis): Kapha obstructs the Pranavaha Srotas, causing Vata (Prana Vayu) to move in a reverse/upward direction (Viloma), leading to breathlessness.
Dosha: Vata and Kapha.
Dushya: Rasa, Prana.
Srotas: Pranavaha, Udakavaha, Annavaha.

[ROGA-007] Prameha
Ayurvedic name: Prameha
English crosswalk: Metabolic / Urinary disorders (includes Diabetes crosswalk)
Nidana (Causes): Asyasukhayam (sedentary lifestyle), Svapnasukhayam (excessive sleep), consuming Dadhi (curd), Gramya-audaka mamsa (meats of domestic/aquatic animals), Navanna (new grains), Guda (jaggery).
Purvarupa (Prodromal): Sweda (excessive sweating), Angagandha (foul body odor), Mutra avilata (turbid urine), Pipasa (thirst), Madhurya of asya (sweet taste in mouth).
Rupa (Symptoms): Prabhuta avila mutrata (frequent and turbid urination).
Samprapti (Pathogenesis): Aggravated Kapha and Pitta mix with Meda (fat) and Kleda, moving downwards to the Mutravaha srotas (urinary tract), corrupting urine.
Dosha: Tridosha (Kapha predominance initially).
Dushya: Meda (fat), Mamsa (muscle), Kleda (body fluids), Rakta, Vasā, Majjā, Lasikā, Rasa, Ojas.
Srotas: Mutravaha, Medovaha.
